The First Principles AI Engine for Chemical Discovery

📢 Announcement: Visit the official hub for our autonomous workflows and agentic AI at the Institute for Strategic AI (theisa.ai) →

Welcome to Lab Initio, the foundational computational engine driving the Institute for Strategic AI (ISA). We build the infrastructure for autonomous scientific discovery. By integrating high-level wavefunction methods and deep-learning DFT with state-of-the-art graph neural networks, we train scientific foundation models purpose-built for chemistry and materials science.

Moving beyond traditional simulation, we deploy these models through an AI-native, autonomous operating model. We connect our predictive and generative AI directly to an ARC-funded self-driving robotic synthesis platform, establishing precision-guided agentic workflows. This autonomous feedback loop designs, tests, and synthesizes new molecules and materials end-to-end without human bottlenecks, dramatically accelerating breakthroughs in renewable energy and applied AI for the physical world.
